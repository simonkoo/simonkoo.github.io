I'm Dr. Koo. On @AIWithKoo, we explore the choices AI puts in our hands: how we learn, treat one another, and share responsibility. We ask whose values count, who sets the rules, and who gets a say. I bring a doctorate in electrical and computer engineering, university teaching experience, and years of exploring and writing about AI ethics and education.
