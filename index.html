Your host

Dr. Koo

Engineer · Educator · Entrepreneur

I'm Dr. Koo. On @AIWithKoo, I use everyday examples, stories, and practical tests to make AI easier to understand. We also explore education, ethics, and the choices and rules that shape its impact on people.

My doctorate in electrical and computer engineering is from Purdue. I studied information engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and operations research at Columbia, and I am a Senior Member of IEEE and ACM. I have also taught at university, written extensively about AI ethics and education, and participated in related standards work.

Research, teaching, and building technology have taught me that understanding how something works matters. So does asking the right questions.
