About Simon

Dr. Simon Koo

古嘉文

Engineer · Educator · Entrepreneur

Simon is a former university professor and technology entrepreneur whose work spans AI ethics and education. He brings experience in teaching, building technology, and participating in AI standards work to the channel.

He is interested in how people learn to use AI, how it influences their decisions, and what responsibility comes with developing and using it. He also approaches these questions as a parent.

Simon holds a PhD in electrical and computer engineering from Purdue. His background also includes information engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and operations research at Columbia. He is a Senior Member of IEEE and ACM.
