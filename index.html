Dr. Simon Koo · 古嘉文

I've taught at universities, built technology ventures, and worked on AI ethics. As a parent, I also care about what these changes mean around the kitchen table.

Here, we bring technical ideas down to earth through stories, evidence, and everyday questions. The aim is to help you understand AI and make your own judgments about it.

I studied information engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and operations research at Columbia, and earned my PhD in electrical and computer engineering at Purdue. That mix shapes how I think about technology, learning, and the choices we make.
